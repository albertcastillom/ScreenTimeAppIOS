**Effective date: October 20, 2026**

Commit: Social App Blocker (the **“App”**) is operated by **Albert Castillo Martinez**. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your information, contact us at **albert.castillom@gmail.com**.

## Information we collect

### Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

- Your email address;
- Your chosen username;
- An internal account identifier; and
- Account creation and authentication information needed to sign you in and maintain your session.

Passwords are handled by our authentication provider. We do not have access to your password in readable form.

### Friends and focus-session information

When you use the App’s social and focus features, we collect information such as:

- Friend requests, friendship status, and the accounts involved;
- Focus-session requests, including the requester, approver, requested duration, status, and relevant timestamps; and
- Focus-session totals used to show session counts and focused time.

Your username may be searchable by other authenticated users. Friends and participants in a focus request can see information needed for that interaction, such as your username, the requested duration, and the request status.

### Screen Time information stored only on your device

If you grant Screen Time permission, you can select apps, app categories, or websites to shield during a focus session. Apple represents these selections using privacy-preserving tokens. The App stores your selection and current blocking state locally in an Apple App Group so the App and its Screen Time extensions can coordinate.

**We do not upload your selected apps, app categories, websites, or Screen Time selection tokens to our servers or share them with friends.** A friend who approves a focus request controls only whether to approve the requested duration; that person cannot see or choose what is blocked on your device.

You may revoke Screen Time authorization in your device settings. You can remove locally stored App data by deleting the App from your device.

### Technical and security information

When the App communicates with our backend provider, limited technical information may be processed automatically, such as IP address, request timestamps, authentication events, and diagnostic or security logs. We use this information to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and prevent abuse of the service.

The current version of the App does not include third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or behavioral advertising. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Create and authenticate accounts;
- Provide username search, friendships, and focus-session requests;
- Start and end focus sessions requested by you;
- Display your profile and focus-session statistics;
- Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the App;
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access;
- Respond to support, privacy, and legal requests; and
- Comply with applicable law and enforce our agreements.

## How we share information

We share information only as described below.

### Other users

We disclose your username and the social or focus-request information necessary to provide features you choose to use. For example, another user can find your username, send you a friend request, and see the duration and status of a focus request involving that user.

### Service providers

We use **Supabase** to provide authentication, database hosting, realtime updates, and account-deletion functions. Supabase processes information on our behalf to provide those services. Supabase may use subprocessors and may process information in countries other than your own. More information is available in [Supabase’s Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy) and [Supabase’s Legal Hub](https://supabase.com/legal).

We also use Apple platform services, including Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity, App Groups, and TestFlight. Apple’s handling of information is governed by its own policies and platform terms.

### Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law or valid legal process, protect the rights and safety of users or others, investigate abuse, or protect the security and integrity of the App.

### Business transfers

If the App or its business is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable law.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain account and feature information while your account remains active and as reasonably necessary to provide the App, maintain security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

You can permanently delete your account from **Profile → Delete Account** in the App. Account deletion removes your authentication account, profile, friendships, and focus requests from the active service. Limited information may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or security logs until those records are deleted through ordinary retention cycles, or longer if required by law.

You may also contact **albert.castillom@gmail.com** for help with an access or deletion request.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. These include authenticated access, encrypted network connections, and database access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## International data transfers

Our service providers may process information in the United States, Singapore, the region selected for our hosted database, and other locations where they or their subprocessors operate. Where required, service providers use contractual and legal safeguards for international transfers.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or a copy of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to appeal a decision or complain to a data-protection authority.

To submit a request, email **albert.castillom@gmail.com**. We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

## Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the App, contact **albert.castillom@gmail.com** so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

If you intend to offer the App to children under 13, do not rely on this section without first implementing appropriate parental-consent and child-privacy measures and obtaining legal advice.
